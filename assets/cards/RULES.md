# Kura Cards: Special Card Rules

## Deck contents
- 4 colors: green, brown, purple, magenta
- Each color: 0-9, Shield, Swap, Shell
- Wild cards: Wild (color change) and Wild +4
- Card back

## Number cards (0-9)
Play a number card when its color or number matches the top card.

## Special cards

**Swap**
Swap 1 random card with an opponent of your choice.

**Shell**
Requires at least 3 cards in your hand, counting the Shell itself.
1. Play the Shell onto the discard pile.
2. Choose 1 card from your hand and an opponent.
3. Give that card to the opponent. It becomes theirs permanently and is not returned.

**Shield**
Can be played like a normal card, matching the color of the top card.
When you are hit by a +4 and you hold a Shield, you may choose to play it:
- The +4 bounces back to the attacker.
- You are not skipped.
- A bounced +4 cannot be bounced again.

**Wild**
Works like UNO: play it anytime and choose the next color.

**Wild +4**
Choose a color. The next player draws 4 cards and is skipped.
